Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

TV Cast is built to send your content from your iPhone to your TV, and nowhere else.

Overview

The short version

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't collect your name or contact details.
  • Your photos, videos, music and screen go directly from your iPhone to your TV over your Wi‑Fi. We never upload them to our servers.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in TV Cast.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Information TV Cast uses

01 / 08

Photos, videos and music. With your permission, TV Cast reads the items you choose from your Photo Library and Music Library so it can play them on your TV. This happens on your device and over your local network only.

Your screen. When you start Screen Mirroring, iOS records your screen and TV Cast streams it to the TV you selected on your local network. Mirroring only runs after you tap Start Broadcast, and iOS shows a red indicator while it's on.

Local network. TV Cast scans your Wi‑Fi network to find compatible TVs and streaming devices and to talk to them. This information stays on your network.

App settings. Choices such as your language and the last TV you connected to are stored only on your device.

Information we do not collect

02 / 08

TV Cast does not collect personal information, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and does not build a profile about you. We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Purchases

03 / 08

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To unlock Pro, TV Cast checks your purchase status with Apple on your device. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

04 / 08

If you report a problem from the app, your email app opens with a message you can review before sending. It includes the problem you chose, the TV details you typed, the app version, your iOS version and your iPhone model. We use this only to answer you and fix the issue, and we delete it when it's no longer needed.

Third‑party services

05 / 08

To connect to TVs, TV Cast uses casting technologies provided by TV and platform makers. These run on your device and your local network, and their analytics are turned off.

The built‑in browser loads websites you choose, such as YouTube. Those websites may collect information under their own privacy policies.

Children

06 / 08

TV Cast is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

07 / 08

You can turn off Photos, Music or Local Network access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security. Some features stop working until you allow access again. Deleting the app removes all data it stored on your device.

Changes

08 / 08

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
