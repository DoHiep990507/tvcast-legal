About TV Cast01 / 05
What is TV Cast?

TV Cast lets you show photos, videos and music from your iPhone on your TV, play web videos on the big screen and mirror your iPhone screen, all over your home Wi‑Fi with no cables or extra setup.

Is TV Cast free?

Yes. You can download TV Cast and use every feature for free with a daily limit. TV Cast Pro removes the limits. There are no ads in the app.

Is TV Cast safe to use?

Yes. TV Cast connects your iPhone and your TV directly over your own Wi‑Fi network. Your photos, videos and screen are never uploaded to our servers, and the app has no ads or tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Getting connected02 / 05
My TV is not showing up. What should I check?

Your iPhone and your TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi network. Then try these steps:

  1. Turn the TV on and make sure it is connected to Wi‑Fi, not only to cable or an antenna.
  2. Allow Local Network access: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → TV Cast.
  3. Turn off any VPN on your iPhone and avoid guest networks, which often block devices from seeing each other.
  4. Restart the TV and the Wi‑Fi router, then reopen TV Cast.
Which TVs work with TV Cast?

TV Cast works with most smart TVs, streaming sticks and media boxes that support common casting standards, as well as AirPlay‑compatible TVs. If your TV shows up in the device list, you can cast to it.

Some older TVs only support a few video formats. If a video won't play, try a different video or update your TV's software.

Why does TV Cast ask for Local Network access?

Local Network access lets TV Cast find TVs on your Wi‑Fi and send your photos, videos and screen to them directly. Without it, the device list stays empty. Nothing is sent outside your home network.

Casting03 / 05
How do I cast photos and videos?
  1. Connect to your TV from the Home screen.
  2. Tap Photos or Videos and allow access to your library.
  3. Tap an item to show it on the TV. Use the controls at the bottom to play, pause or move between items.
Why can't I find some songs?

TV Cast can only play songs that are saved on this iPhone. Songs that are only streamed, or that are protected by copyright (DRM), can't be cast.

How do I play web videos on my TV?

Open YouTube on the Home screen, start playing a video in the built‑in browser, then tap Cast to TV. Some websites block casting, and some videos may not play on every TV.

The video is choppy or keeps buffering. What can I do?
  • Move closer to the Wi‑Fi router, or use the 5 GHz network if your router has one.
  • Close other apps that use a lot of data, such as downloads or video calls.
  • Large or high‑resolution videos need a strong connection. Try a shorter or smaller video.
Screen mirroring04 / 05
How do I mirror my iPhone screen?

Apple TV or AirPlay TVs: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your TV. Enter the code shown on the TV if asked.

Other TVs:

  1. Connect to the TV in TV Cast and tap Screen Mirroring.
  2. Choose Smooth or Sharp, then tap Start Mirroring.
  3. In the popup, tap Start Broadcast. Your TV opens the stream by itself.
What is the difference between Smooth and Sharp?

Smooth shows your screen with very little delay, which is best for apps, games and presentations. It shows the picture only, without sound, and your TV needs a web browser.

Sharp sends a clearer video stream with sound, but runs a few seconds behind. It's best for watching videos.

How do I stop mirroring?

Tap the red recording indicator at the top of your screen and choose Stop, or open TV Cast and tap Stop Mirroring.

Subscription05 / 05
What does TV Cast Pro include?

Pro removes the daily limits on casting photos, videos, music and web videos, and unlocks HD screen mirroring.

How does the free trial work?

If a plan includes a free trial, you won't be charged during the trial. When it ends, your subscription starts automatically at the price shown before you subscribed, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TV Cast and tap Cancel Subscription, or open Settings → Manage Subscription in TV Cast. You keep Pro until the end of the current period.

I bought Pro but it isn't active. What should I do?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then tap Restore on the Pro screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
